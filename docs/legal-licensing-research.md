# Legal & Licensing Research: Tourist Intermediation in the Canary Islands

**Company:** EXCURSION PHOTO SERVICE, S.L. (CIF: B38638060)
**Current Status:** Registered as a photo studio.
**Activity:** Acting as an intermediary via `tenjet.club`, taking bookings for jet ski safaris via WhatsApp and passing them to operators for a commission.

## 1. Can a company registered for photographic activities legally act as a booking intermediary for tourist excursions?
**No.** Under Canary Islands Law (*Ley 7/1995, de 6 de abril, de Ordenación del Turismo de Canarias* and *Decreto 89/2010*), to mediate or organize tourist services, the entity must be legally registered as an "Intermediador Turístico" (Tourist Intermediary) or "Agencia de Viajes" (Travel Agency). 

The current photo studio license only covers photographic activities. It does not provide legal cover to act as a commission-based intermediary for tourist excursions. To act as an intermediary legally, the company must file a "Declaración Responsable de Intermediación Turística" before the local authorities (such as the Cabildo de Tenerife) and be inscribed in the *Registro General de Empresas, Actividades y Establecimientos Turísticos*.

## 2. Do they need to register under a specific IAE or CNAE code?
**Yes.** Operating as a tourist intermediary requires adding the specific economic activity codes to the company's tax and mercantile registry:
- **IAE (Impuesto sobre Actividades Económicas):** The correct code is **Epígrafe 755 (Agencias de Viajes)**, which covers travel agencies, intermediaries, and tour organizers. 
- **CNAE:** The appropriate code is **7911 (Actividades de las agencias de viajes)** or **7990 (Otros servicios de reservas y actividades relacionadas con los mismos)**.

## 3. What are the risks/fines of operating without updating the IAE and Tourism Registration?
The risks of operating an unregistered tourist intermediary service are severe:
- **Tourism Fines (Ley 7/1995):** Operating a tourist activity without the mandatory inscription in the *Registro General* is classified as an **infracción muy grave** (very serious infraction). The economic fines for very serious infractions range from **30,001 € to 300,000 €** (Article 78 & 79 of Ley 7/1995). The administration can also order the temporary suspension of business activities.
- **Tax/Hacienda Penalties:** Emitting invoices for tourist commissions under a photo studio IAE code can lead to tax penalties for failing to update census data (typically 250 € - 400 € per infraction) and could trigger a tax audit, especially since travel intermediaries often fall under the special VAT regime for travel agencies (REAV).

## 4. Temporary legal disclaimers to mitigate risk
While waiting to finalize the proper legal registration, the website should include prominent disclaimers to argue that it acts only as an advertising board rather than an intermediary. *(Note: This is a temporary stopgap and does not offer complete legal protection if authorities investigate the commission/booking structure).*

The website must add a **"Descargo de Responsabilidad de Intermediación" (Intermediation Disclaimer)** stating:
- EXCURSION PHOTO SERVICE, S.L. acts strictly as an **advertising directory and communication channel**, not as a travel agency, tour operator, or tourist intermediary.
- The company does not organize, sell, or assume liability for the jet ski excursions.
- The contractual relationship for the excursion is established directly and exclusively between the user and the final jet ski operator.
- Any reservation requests facilitated via WhatsApp are merely forwarded to the final operator, who is solely responsible for the execution, safety, pricing, and legality of the activity.
