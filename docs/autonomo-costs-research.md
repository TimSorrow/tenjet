# Costs of Setting Up as an Autónomo (Tourist Intermediary) in the Canary Islands

Operating as a Tourist Intermediary (*Intermediador Turístico*) in the Canary Islands involves specific regional advantages (like the "Cuota Cero" and IGIC) but also strict regulatory requirements depending on the exact nature of the intermediary work.

Below is a detailed breakdown of the setup and recurring costs to calculate a realistic monthly "burn rate."

## 1. Initial Setup Costs
*   **Declaración Responsable de Intermediación Turística:** The submission of this declaration to the Canary Islands Government via their electronic headquarters is **free of direct administrative fees (tasas)**.
*   **Gestoría Setup Fees:** Many gestorías charge a one-time setup fee to register you with Hacienda (Census models), Social Security (RETA), and to process the Declaración Responsable. This usually ranges from **€50 to €150**.
*   **Financial Guarantees (Caution):** If your intermediation involves selling "Linked Travel Arrangements" (servicios de viaje vinculados) or "Package Travels" (viajes combinados), the law requires a substantial financial guarantee (fianza), often starting around **€100,000**. If you are a *pure intermediary* (just connecting buyers and sellers without creating packages or handling the funds in a way that constitutes a travel package), this guarantee may not apply. 

## 2. Monthly Recurring Costs (Burn Rate)
To keep the Autónomo status active, you will face strict monthly fixed costs, even if you bill €0.

*   **Cuota de Autónomos (Social Security):** 
    *   **Standard Tarifa Plana:** New autónomos pay a reduced flat rate (Tarifa Plana) of approximately **€80 - €86 per month** for the first 12 months (extendable to 24 months if net income is below the minimum wage).
    *   **Canary Islands "Cuota Cero":** The Canary Islands offer a "Zero Quota" subsidy. However, **this is not an upfront discount**. You still must pay the ~€86/month out of pocket. At the end of the 12-month period, you apply to the Canary Islands Government for a full reimbursement of those quotas. 
    *   *Cash Flow Impact:* Expect to burn **€86/month** upfront.
*   **Gestoría / Accounting Fees:** A local gestoría experienced in the Canary Islands tax regime (IGIC, Model 420) is highly recommended. For basic services (quarterly tax filings and basic bookkeeping), prices range from **€45 to €70 per month**. More comprehensive services run up to €120/month.
    *   *Cash Flow Impact:* Average **€60/month**.

## 3. Mandatory Insurance Costs
*   **Seguro de Responsabilidad Civil (RC):** 
    *   **Is it mandatory?** For a *pure* tourist intermediary (e.g., an online portal or basic referral agent), a general RC insurance is not strictly mandated by the autonomy's specific tourism law, unlike the financial guarantees. However, it is **highly recommended**. 
    *   **Exception (Active Tourism):** If your intermediation heavily involves or bleeds into organizing **Turismo Activo** (adventure, water sports, hiking), Canary Islands law strictly mandates both RC insurance and accident/rescue insurance. 
    *   **Estimated Cost:** For a standard intermediary/minor travel agency risk profile, an annual RC policy typically costs between **€250 and €400 per year** (approx. €20 - €33/month). If active tourism is involved, it can jump significantly.

## 4. Taxes
*   **IGIC vs. IVA:** In the Canary Islands, you will apply the Impuesto General Indirecto Canario (IGIC) instead of the mainland IVA. The standard IGIC rate is **7%** (compared to 21% IVA). This makes services cheaper for end consumers but requires specific local tax filings (Modelo 420). If you qualify, you might opt into the REPEP (Régimen Especial del Pequeño Empresario o Profesional), which exempts you from charging IGIC if your annual billing is under €30,000, simplifying paperwork.
*   **IRPF (Income Tax):** 
    *   **B2B (Invoicing businesses):** You must apply an IRPF retention on your invoices. For new autónomos, this is **7%** for the first three years (then 15%). You don't pay this monthly; it's deducted from what your client pays you.
    *   **B2C (Invoicing individuals):** No retention on invoices. Instead, you pay **20% of your net profits** every quarter via Modelo 130 as an advance payment on your annual income tax.

## Summary: Realistic Monthly Burn Rate
To simply keep the status alive as a pure intermediary (assuming no office rent and no travel packages that require the €100k guarantee):

*   **Social Security:** ~€86 (Reimbursed yearly via Cuota Cero, but requires upfront cash flow)
*   **Gestoría:** ~€60
*   **RC Insurance (Pro-rated):** ~€25 
*   **Software/Misc (Invoicing tool, bank fees):** ~€15
*   **Total Expected Monthly Cash Burn:** **~€186 per month** 

This is the absolute minimum cash you need in the bank each month just to maintain the legal structure, before any marketing, web hosting, or salary expenses.
